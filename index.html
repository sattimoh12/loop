The Search Model

The cycle

tighten (name the wall, criterion sharp) → release (fog up, value unbinds from action) → generate → recapture (the flinch flags a candidate — evaluate the batch now, fully) → link (abstract the parts noun-free; the map station) → close (finish or park, deposit either way) → tighten again.

Inside generate, its own engine — the piston:

dip (one novelty probed to threshold — a fixed anchor) → jump (the long reach, aimed by the abstracted wall) → dip (colonize the landing until the borrowed move is owned) → jump again. F s41467-026-68639-2 — the dip and the directed jump co-express in the best performers, in that order

Each revolution of the piston extends the map by one owned region. Ranging without dipping = many contacts, no anchors. Dipping without jumping = persistence, never leaves the region.

Feeding the whole machine, a supply line that is not a phase — the intake:

a constant stream of inputs from outside your lane — books, paintings, films, music, rooms — because you can only jump to places you know. The map's reach is capped by what's been taken in; no dial setting substitutes for territory that isn't there. Intake obeys the production chain like everything else: what's taken in without a link sentence is leisure (allowed — label it); what leaves one sentence — this and my work share X — is map. Deep immersion in narrative is not a break from the work; it runs on the same machinery the work does. A

One motion, run at three timescales: minutes (inner loop), weeks (outer loop, per piece), years (season loop, across pieces). The value is produced by the pass through, not by any setting held — every level-based result in the corpus shows an inverted-U, every time-based result shows this sequence; the same finding viewed statically and dynamically. C the unification; the halves are sourced below

Getting better means four things, separately: deeper releases (how far the jumps travel), cleaner recaptures (a real selection event with output, never mixed into generation), flipping on the signal (the flinch and the click, not the clock, not discomfort), and running all three timescales on purpose.

One signal governs the flip at every scale: is something accumulating? Only rejections → park / finish with debts / stay in breadth. Partial structure → persist / revise / commit.

A vocabulary for indexing your own practice. Not a causal theory — no result could arrive and make “fog” correct, because fog isn’t a claim. The test is whether the categories cut your work at joints you can act on differently.

Marks: F results read · A abstract only · P perspective/review · C my claim, no source.

Four parts

Compasswhat to value. An open problem you refuse to close.
Foghow loudly value binds to action. A waveform, not a level — both flat settings are wrong. Evaluating every line collapses you onto the obvious; never evaluating underperforms.
Mapwhat’s reachable.

Compass and fog are the content and the volume of one signal. Compass and map aren’t fully independent — a wall that won’t abstract is unreachable regardless of density.

The production chain — four stations, four products. Nothing skips a station:

FOG makes oreraw candidates, and noticed flinches (banked, not acted on). Fog’s products are experiences — fog never writes.
EVALUATION makes partsthe pick, failure lines, candidate compasses. Everything containing a verdict is made here, out of fog’s ore. This is the compass factory.
LINKING makes maprung-3 sentences, bridges, axes — by abstracting evaluation’s parts. A failure line is a part; its noun-free generalization is map.
CLOSE makes objectsfinished things — the only items a criterion can test against.

This resolves the recurring drift: “compasses come from fog” and “compasses come from evaluation” describe adjacent stations on one line — fog supplies the material, evaluation manufactures the compass. Even étude learning-lines obey the chain: noticed during play, written at the session-end mini-recapture. Anything written is downstream of fog.

Two routes

Both produce creative work. A

Flexibilitybroad search, switching across categories. Everything below is built for it.
Persistencesystematic, effortful, incremental probing within few categories. Sustained work is the mechanism, not a symptom. Generate-judge-generate is legitimate here — it is this route — when partial structure is accumulating.

The stop rule stays, with a discriminator in front of it.

Park it after ~25 minutes C the number — unless something is actually accumulating. Only rejected candidates piling up → insight route. Park it. Partial structure piling up, the thing measurably further along → persistence route. Continue.

Provenance: the stop rule came from an insight-task result and its transfer was poor. It stays as an aimed corrective — the drift it opposes is revising past the peak and never closing. False as theory, correct as correction.

Compass

Three tests on the sentence

1 · ExcludeRun it against a bounded set and count survivors. >30% is a topic · 5–30% works · <5% is too tight. C thresholds are calibration guesses; the direction is not
2 · DirectDo you know what to do in the next five minutes?
3 · TerminateEither can you say what would count as solved, or would you recognise it — the second only where you’ve compared fifty of that kind. Recognition without training is stopping when tired, and it feels identical from inside.

One test on the outcome

4 · VerifySolve the stated conflict, then return to the original dissatisfaction. Flinch still there → the wall was misnamed. Back to naming, not to bridging. Verify applies to batch-picks too: “best of the batch” without verify can crown the least-bad near candidate.

Shapes that work: a conflict between two requirements, or a requirement the thing must meet. Every exit line makes her cruel or weak, and she can be neither.

Shapes that don’t: a topic (excludes nothing) · an aspiration (no next move) · a judgment (it isn’t original enough — nothing named, nothing to violate).

The third is a portrait: only an over-evaluator generates verdicts where a compass should be.

Not original enough decomposes on one push: every route I have to this effect is one of about four moves, and I’ve used all four. Name the four.

The failure mode is closing early with a plausible answer, not vagueness. A compass that resolves prematurely stops working, silently.

The dial is graded, and each height is a different instrument:

Low

Dosing rule: match height to wall difficulty — high fog on an easy wall adds distraction and slows you down F s44271-025-00235-4, optimal exploration capacity scales with difficulty. Sharpness sets fog tolerance: how high the dial can go while the compass survives is set by how sharp the wall statement is. To fog harder, sharpen the wall — never the admissible solution space. The height profile of a session is a craft object: mid until candidates repeat, spike high for the far jumps, drop for recapture.

A working implementation of delay C the rule F the mechanism: value counts only on the Nth try — e.g., nothing before the 15th candidate counts. This reproduces the delayed-reward condition that produced directed exploration where per-choice reward erased it s41467-026-68639-2. Caution: if you catch yourself counting (“that was 7 of 15”), the counting has become the evaluator’s job — use a page-count or timer instead.

Why not generate-judge-generate (on the insight route): each judgment re-binds value to the next choice (the SNR mechanism — you can’t hold the gain down while repeatedly exercising it), and each judgment resets the walk to length one — distance can only accumulate over unjudged steps, and insight tracks cumulative distance. Tested directly: per-choice evaluation didn’t reduce directed exploration, it erased it. F The judgment isn’t lost in batching — it’s deferred, then done fully. Deferring is not suppressing: a mid-stream flinch (too on the nose) gets noted and banked, not argued with — that’s delay keeping the criterion available; occlusion would mean never forming it.

Four mechanisms that behave differently

Delaysignal intact, action blocked until the end — the only one with a direct creative-task result
Occludenever form the judgment; can’t recover it — untested
Attenuatelower the gain on signal→action — bandit tasks, but the drift-rate/SNR result (s41598-021-82530-8) is this mechanism; strongest process-level anchor in the corpus
Degradedamage the comparator — load, fatigue. Costly, different operation

The check: can you still reject? Put a candidate up and see if you get a differential reaction. Indifference means gain is at zero and you’re drifting.

Fog is not fatigue. Attentional lapses are local sleep-like waves — degraded control, not lowered gain. A s41467-021-23890-7 A tired session that skips evaluation is drift with the comparator offline. Rested is a precondition.

The deep-dip

(v1.1 called this “chunking”; renamed — the memory sense of chunking is the result, not the act.)

When one move flickers during generation — the trigger is a double flicker, the same move arriving twice unbidden — stop ranging and probe that move repeatedly: five or so variations in a row. Then return to ranging.

Whyexplorers who probed one novelty to a threshold before ranging showed stronger directed exploration afterward and better outcomes than pure rangers — the behaviours co-express; it isn’t a trade. F s41467-026-68639-2 A jump measured from a stable landmark is informative; a jump from a blur is noise.
Lengthuntil you could state what the move buys and what it costs — roughly, when you can predict the next variant’s quality before writing it.
Endback to ranging or to evaluation. The dip must not become the session.
Depositone sentence — residue works when X, fails when Y. A deep-dip that leaves no sentence spent the reps. (The five probes compress into one known unit with edges — this is where the memory sense of chunking is manufactured.)
The corrective it aims atthe switching-biased failure mode of ten one-touch contacts, nothing stabilized. Try the weird thing five times before trying five weird things.

Map

Density — many links inside a category, from depth-first study and deep-dips. Bridges — a few long edges between distant regions. A bridge is an edge; it’s just a long one. Size isn’t the variable.

Distance is measured on your map, not the world’s taxonomy. Two consequences: within-domain far jumps exist (your own periphery, dead conventions, inverted assumptions — check it before reaching for a new domain; it’s usually not exhausted, just unfashionable to you). And a well-built second domain stops being far — the point of fifty-compared is that it installs permanent bridges, converting a class of far jumps into near moves and relocating the frontier. A new domain is where far jumps come from before the bridge exists; after, it’s infrastructure.

Easy fog grows density; hard walls grow bridges. They come from different sessions.

You can only bridge to places you know. One domain means every solution comes from inside it, which is what unoriginal actually means.

Linking

The ladder: attribute (both are wary of people — generates nothing) → relation (the individual is expendable to the group) → process (order maintained by never naming the threat).

Function is a separate axis, not a fourth rung. For structural problems, rung 3 is the target.

Test 1: strip the nouns. Survives with both things removed → above rung one. Test 2: does it tell you what happens in scene four? A real abstraction creates obligations.

Push étude learnings up the ladder before writing them down. “Stillness at the top of a scene adds tension” is noun-bound; “tension is an expected discharge, withheld” deposits ten edges instead of one.

Sandbox learnings are hypotheses. A principle from wall-less play has unknown failure conditions. Pipeline: étude proposes → the next draft deploys → the failure line, if it comes, is the verified deposit. Play proposes; the piece disposes.

Other ways to link: shared function · relational analogy · contrast (same except in one respect — how axes are derived) · causal · boundary condition.

What to bridge between: your abstracted wall and a domain where that abstraction is already solved. A bridge without a wall is decoration — when solving. Wall-less linking that deposits sentences is map work, not decoration; the decoration charge applies to bridging as a substitute for solving.

The loop

Inner — minutes, many times per piece

name the wall as a conflict → fog up → batch (deep-dip the double-flicker, link-in-fog: abstract the wall and range toward domains where it’s solved) → evaluate the batch at once → verify the pick → link the parts (push failure lines and link sentences up the ladder) → close the beat (fixed, or parked with the placeholder version written) → gap → next wall

1Attempt it straight, rules on, until genuinely stuck.
2Name the wall as a conflict.
3Fog, aimed at the wall, height matched to difficulty. Delay evaluation to the end of the sequence (the Nth-try rule).
4Deep-dip whatever flickers twice; then range far — linking as a generation move: abstract the wall until noun-free, jump via a domain where that abstraction is already solved.
5Evaluate the batch. Verify the pick against the original dissatisfaction.
6Link the parts: the failure lines and link sentences get pushed up the ladder before banking — one noun-free sentence deposits ten edges. (Linking appears twice in this loop: inside fog as the bridge move, here as the abstraction station.)
7Close: fix, or park with the failure line written — parking on a bare verdict banks nothing. The failure lines are next session’s compasses.
8Gap — a real break after a real attempt, not filled with something demanding.

Outer — weeks, once per piece

piece compass (what this piece is for) → draft whole, straight through — walls marked in one bracket line and skipped, nothing solved mid-stream → return pass: triage → inner loops on the real walls only → your own verdict, said aloud → the room → finish → next piece

Mark, don’t solve, mid-draftOne bracket naming the flinch — [sound too hot for the curve] — and keep moving. Naming is allowed mid-stream; solving is not.
The finished draft changes what the walls areHalf the beats that felt broken read fine in context; the verify test needs the whole object. This is why scene-first isn’t just permitted but better.
Triagecheap fixes in-line (that is finishing) · coupled walls — fix the one whose failure explains the most others; expect some to dissolve · real walls (only rejections accumulate) get the inner loop — capped at one deep loop per scene per pass.
Ship with debtsEvery other real wall gets the placeholder version written, the failure line banked, and the scene closes anyway. A scene closed with two known debts is finished in every way that matters; a scene held open until every beat is right is where neither play nor finishing happens.
Fatten the ends, starve the middleplay lives in the draft, finishing lives in the close; the two things that are neither are per-candidate judgment during generation and open-ended wall-grinding during revision.

Season — years, across pieces

breadth (pieces that deliberately differ) → the catch (one direction produces differential flinch AND accumulating structure — the stop-rule discriminator at career scale) → commitment (the next N pieces exploit it, narrowing hard)

The one career-scale result: streaks of high-impact work begin at the transition from exploration to committed exploitation — neither phase alone beats baseline. F s41467-021-25477-8 Cannot be run per-piece; it is run across them.

The loops nest by feeding closes upward: closed beats make pieces finishable; finished pieces are the only things a season can compare; the season’s catch re-aims the next piece compasses.

The training

This is habit formation, not deliberate practice.

Deliberate practice needs a well-defined task, immediate informative feedback, repetition with error correction. Don’t judge mid-stream has none of those. And the deliberate-practice numbers run the wrong way: ~26% of variance in games, 21% music, 18% sports, 4% education, under 1% professions. Directing is the unstructured end. A

What fits is habit: a behavioural rule, in a consistent context, on a cue. Median ~66 days to automaticity, range 18 to 254, cue consistency the main driver, missing a single day doesn’t break the curve. A

  • Repetition-in-context predicts acquisition. Comprehension doesn’t. Rereading this document does nothing. Running the loop does.
  • A quarter is roughly the right probe length.
  • Missing days cost nothing — no streaks anywhere in this.

To make fog trainable, give it an error signal. One binary at session end: did I evaluate mid-stream — yes or no. Scored on behaviour, not output. Rested sessions only — a tired session skipping evaluation is drift, not fog, and logging it corrupts the curve. Noting-and-banking a flinch does not count as evaluating; acting on it does.

The daily practice

One message a day, to one person. The voice memo, one link sentence, one failure line. A folder is self-imposed; a person isn’t.

Intake runs continuously and cheaply: something from outside your lane always in progress; the only rule is one link sentence per finished intake — this and my work share X — or it was leisure, which is fine, labeled. The intake sentence can be the daily message.

Everything else attaches to a date that already exists — predictions to a screening, transcription to the day before a shoot, wall-naming to whenever you’re actually stuck.

Weekly: one delivery of a finished thing to two or three people.

Session shape (typical): draft loose (the play) · one étude (more play) · short return pass · at most one deep wall · deposits out.

Never: reread today’s pages · open old scripts before starting · judge anything mid-draft (mark it and move).

Two probes, one quarter

1Does the second deposit change anything? A decade of sentences-without-closure is the baseline. Run closure for a quarter and compare.
2Is fog trainable? The binary, logged at session end, rested sessions only. Yes-rate falls over a quarter → fog has a learning curve. Doesn’t move → fog is a state you enter or don’t.
